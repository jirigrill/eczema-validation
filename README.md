# eczema-validation

Market validation for a commercial variant of the [eczema-ios](https://github.com/jirigrill/eczema-ios) app: does a paying audience exist?

**Private on purpose.** Holds revenue targets, ad-spend experiments, competitor teardowns and an unlaunched brand name — none of which belong in the public app repo.

- The effort is charted as a wayfinder map: [#1](https://github.com/jirigrill/eczema-validation/issues/1). Read it first.
- Research findings live in `docs/research/`, one file per research ticket, every claim cited.
- The landing page will live here too, once the ICP and promise tickets resolve.

**The app repo is unaffected by anything decided here.** The family app ships as specified either way.
