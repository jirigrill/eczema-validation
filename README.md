# eczema-validation

> ## On hold — September 2026
>
> **This work is paused indefinitely, and the pause is not about the findings.** Our son's eczema has cleared. The problem that motivated the app is gone from our family, and staying in the topic longer than necessary carries a cost we are no longer willing to pay.
>
> Nothing here is retracted. The research in `docs/research/` stands as written, every claim still cited, and the map in [#1](https://github.com/jirigrill/eczema-validation/issues/1) still records what was decided and why. It is kept as history, not abandoned as a mistake — anyone picking the commercial question back up, us included, should start from these files rather than from scratch.
>
> No further research tickets will be filed and no landing page will be built. Open issues stay open as an honest record of what was still unanswered.

- The landing page will live here too, once the ICP and promise tickets resolve.
